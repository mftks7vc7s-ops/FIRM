About FIRM

Building the future of reproductive medicine

Progress in reproductive medicine depends on highly trained professionals, ethical scientific innovation, and close cooperation between clinicians, laboratory scientists, educators, researchers, and industry partners.

FIRM exists to expand access to specialized education, strengthen professional competencies, support research, and create meaningful platforms for scientific exchange.

Mission

Advance professional excellence

Improve reproductive medicine through education, research, innovation, leadership, and collaboration.

Vision

Connect science and practice

Build a global professional community equipped to improve reproductive science and healthcare.

Commitment

Lead responsibly

Promote scientific integrity, ethical practice, transparency, inclusion, and evidence-based learning.

Education and Training

Programs for the full reproductive medicine team

Clinical Embryology

Embryo culture, ICSI, biopsy, cryopreservation, quality systems, laboratory leadership, and emerging technologies.

Reproductive Endocrinology

Advanced physician education, multidisciplinary case review, clinical innovation, leadership, and research collaboration.

Andrology and Genetics

Male reproductive health, laboratory andrology, PGT, genomics, molecular diagnostics, and precision medicine.

REI Physicians

Supporting clinical leadership and interdisciplinary excellence

FIRM supports Reproductive Endocrinologists and Infertility specialists through continuing professional education, clinical–laboratory integration, physician leadership, collaborative research, and responsible adoption of emerging technologies.

  • Advanced clinical education and scientific forums
  • Multidisciplinary case conferences
  • Physician leadership and faculty opportunities
  • Clinical and laboratory quality improvement
  • Multicenter and investigator-led research
  • International professional collaboration
